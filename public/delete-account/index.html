Delete your HouseRaja account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.

In the app (instant)

  1. Open HouseRaja and tap the Settings (gear) icon on Home.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete forever.

By email

Can't access the app? Email goelbuilds@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my HouseRaja account". We complete requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your login, name and email, and all services, attendance, payments, expenses and notes — permanently and immediately.

Crash reports (without your records) may remain with Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
