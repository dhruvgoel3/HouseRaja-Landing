HouseRaja

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Overview

HouseRaja ("we", "the app") helps you track household services and daily expenses. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have. Effective 24 November 2026. Developer: Dhruv Goel, India.

Data we collect

Account data: your name and email address, used to create and secure your account.

Content you add: services (for example milkman or maid), rates, attendance, payments, expenses, categories and notes.

Diagnostics: crash reports (device model, Android version, app version and error details) via Firebase Crashlytics.

Usage analytics: basic in-app events (for example "expense added") via Firebase Analytics. We do not collect your Android advertising ID.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, SMS or bank details, and we never ask for card or UPI information.

How we use it

To provide the app: storing your records, syncing them across your devices and calculating bills and totals.

To keep your account secure and to send account emails (verification and password reset).

To find and fix crashes and improve the app. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Mumbai region, India).

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest on Google servers (AES-256).

Database security rules allow each account to read and write only its own data.

On your phone, data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's device encryption, and excluded from device backups.

Sharing

We share data only with Google Firebase, which processes it on our behalf to run the app, and when required by law.

When you use "Share bill" or "Share summary", the text is shared only with the app and person you choose.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all its data anytime in Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

You can also request deletion by emailing goelbuilds@gmail.com. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for up to 90 days and analytics events according to Google's standard retention, without your records.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can access, correct and erase your data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Contact goelbuilds@gmail.com — we respond within 30 days.

Children

HouseRaja is intended for adults (18+) managing a household. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.

Questions or grievances: goelbuilds@gmail.com.
